How We Work

The Approach

Every engagement starts with the data. We map what you have, identify what's missing, and build from there — no templates, no generic frameworks applied out of context.

01
Diagnose

We audit your existing data, identify gaps, and map your reporting obligations and strategic objectives. You get a clear picture of where you stand before we recommend anything.

02
Design

We build a strategy tailored to your organisation — data collection architecture, framework selection, carbon boundary, or decarbonisation roadmap. Practical and proportionate to your size and sector.

03
Deliver

We execute alongside your team — producing reports, carbon inventories, LCAs, and stakeholder communications that are audit-ready and built to last beyond the engagement.

About

Built on solid ground.

Maati means soil — the foundation things grow from. It's the right name for a firm that believes sustainability strategy has to be grounded: in your actual data, your sector's real constraints, and the regulatory landscape your business operates in.

We work with organisations across industries — manufacturing, finance, technology, food and beverage, consumer goods — helping them build sustainability practices that are credible, defensible, and tied to business outcomes.

We say what it is, not what sounds good. That's what makes the work credible.

Our approach is rigorous by design. We don't apply generic frameworks or produce reports that sit on a shelf. We embed in your process, work with your data, and deliver outputs that your auditors, investors, and leadership teams can rely on.

The Team

Who you'll work with

Dewika Bhagwat
Founder · Sustainability & Impact Consultant

Dewika has over eight years of experience across sustainability strategy, ESG data, and impact advisory — including four years in enterprise sustainability consulting. She has led regulatory reporting engagements across BRSR, CSRD, TCFD, SASB, and GRI for clients in manufacturing, finance, technology, and consumer goods, and calculates end-to-end carbon inventories including Scope 3 and financed emissions.


Before founding Maati Impact, she worked at Novata on ESG data for private equity portfolios, and spent four years at Zomato leading growth strategy across new verticals — where she ran the company's first sustainable packaging campaign, which led her to study corporate sustainability at Columbia University (M.S. Sustainability Management, GPA 3.9).
